Artist Biography

I am Wilson Ekelem, a Motion Designer based in Lagos, working in the Advertising Industry.

Specializing in Adobe After Effects and Cinema 4D, Wilson transforms brand briefs into tactile 3D animations, energetic kinetic typography, product visualizations, and commercial spots for leading brands and festivals across Nigeria.
